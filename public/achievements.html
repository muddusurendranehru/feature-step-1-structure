🧮 Our Core Formula

TyG Index — The Root Cause Test

The TyG (Triglyceride-Glucose) Index is the most powerful, low-cost surrogate for Insulin Resistance. Dr. Surendra was among the first physicians in India to use HOMA-IR and TyG Index together — catching diabetes, PCOS, fatty liver, and hypertension at the root, years before symptoms appear.

TyG INDEX FORMULA
ln [TG (mg/dL) × FBG (mg/dL) / 2]
HOMA-IR FORMULA
Fasting Insulin × Fasting Glucose / 405
